Here is my first model, and almost the only one, it's a tyrannosaur with a rig and a walk animation. I think the mesh is nice, but the animation is a bit poor and the texture is a disaster. I didn't do it for a particular project, just to learn, then I tryed to export it to unity which was straightforward.

![wireframeDino](/images/blog/first-3d-model/wireframeDino.png)

 Before Blender, I tryed 3Dsmax and Modo, but they all got interfaces worse than the others, I ended on blender, it was clearly not my favourite, but the 3D association of Epitech helped me to learn, without them, I would still be fighting to extrude and subdivide a cube :).

Actually, it's not just about knowing how to extrude and move vertices, graphists has "design pattern", somewhat. In theory, once you know how to declare a variable, use a "if and a "while", you can program anything, in practice it's often more complicated, many times I spoke to beginner which know everything but just repeat "I don't see how to do", either I never felt that in front of a "void main() {}", either that's exactly how I always feel in front of a "cube"
