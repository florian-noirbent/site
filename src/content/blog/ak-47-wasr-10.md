I don't have much time to make games at the moment, so I went back to 3D modeling. I started with a classic: a real-world weapon, for which there is a huge amount of photos and existing models to use as reference. Coming up with a science-fiction weapon from scratch seems a lot harder. So here is an AK-47, the WASR-10 variant (a Kalashnikov):

![Render of the AK-47 WASR-10 model](/images/blog/ak-47-wasr-10/AK47_screenshoot.jpg)

It took around 12 hours: 6 for modeling, 3 for UV unwrapping (flattening the mesh's surface into 2D so a texture can be painted onto it) and 3 for texturing. I have no benchmark to tell whether that is fast, but I suspect I'm still slow. Compared to the [TurboSquid models](http://www.turbosquid.com/3d-model/mikhail-kalashnikov/ak-47), it holds up against the best free ones, and maybe even the "serious" paid ones up to $10 in the same polygon range.

![Side view and wireframe of the AK-47 model](/images/blog/ak-47-wasr-10/Photo-QQ20141031211404-1024x313.jpg)

![Another render of the AK-47 model](/images/blog/ak-47-wasr-10/AK47_screenshoot2.jpg)

I decided to share this model *(the download link was hosted on my old website and the file could not be recovered)*. The package includes the .blend and .fbx files plus the textures (diffuse, specular and the UV layout). As for the license: do whatever you want with it :)
