This is a more recent project here since I presented it less than a month ago.

As its name doesn't implies, it's a project I worked on the whole school year, it started in october to end in april. because it's a big project, i'll present it in several articles (at least 3). It was supposed to be a 6 person project, but I worked almost alone, my mate made some other projects without me in the same time.

This first article will show the concept and the final result, I'll talk about algorithm, problems, optimizations, etc in the next. rather than describe, here is a video:

(don't forget to turn English subtitle on !)

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/MZ8s6v2yHSY" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allow="accelerometer; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>

to summarize:

- a huge map editor (which represent hal of the working time), using a module system.

- procedurally generated dungeons from modules

- 2 playable class (ranged and melee)

- Experience and leveling, then distribution of points between 5 branch: power, health, resist, attack speed, move speed

- some levels in platform perspective

This may look small for a "work of a year", but thinking about the project choice took several weeks, then the editor was made from November to January, the game was actually only made from February to March, the beginning of April was for adding the multiplayer mode, and it wasn't a full time job, a lot of other projects was in the same time, and I couldn't afford to spend more than a day per weeks to it. So the game, as you can see it, was only made in fifteen programming days, including the learning of Xbox API.
