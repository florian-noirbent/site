Almost 4 months without new articles, I will unlikely be able to resume my previous rythm before mid-summer.

I'm showing today the illustrating prototype made with my game design concept for the game design course of this year. The game is called Captain Kim, related to the game's pitch.

It's inspired by [Spacewar](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spacewar_(video_game))! (1961) and [Asteroid](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asteroids_(video_game))(1979). the concept is simple, asteroids arrives on Earth, the ship as to destroy them before their impacts, in the GDC, there is also aliens but they are not aviable in this prototype, it's also supposed to work by waves of ennemy
