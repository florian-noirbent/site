Here i am, after 3 messy days putting things together, the blog only wait for my articles.

To quickly introduce myself, Florian Noirbent, I'm a third years student at Epitech (I should start saying 4th now) at Epitech, I know C, C++ and C# programming, and I know to use openGL, Ogre, XNA, but like nearly everyone now, I'm a Unity fan.

First web site, with the discovery of the magic world of back-offices, the forest of interdependent plugins and for most of them are not up to date, it remind me a lot the world of warcraft GUI's plugins actually. I don't know anything about php yet, the "$" and "=>" everywhere are disconcerting, but i'm sure in several months, I'll write my own cool plugins (or update those I just installed I should say...), it's like lua of a certain game, where I ended spending more rewriting my chat and my minimap than killing ugly things.

For now I have a plugin allowing me to write my article in multiple language, because no one speaks french, and a plugin allowing me to post unity web players, to easily share every concept that cross my mind.

## Why a blog ?

I'll share my work here, starting by the elders in the first articles, nobody want to see screenshots of a pong, so I'll be kick on that, then once I catch up the three years late of the blog, I'll show every weeks my work, with video, web player, and a few lines of text. I'll share some of my ridiculous try of 3D modeling (no way to hide it, you'll see it anyway in my games >&lt; until I meet a true graphic artist to follow me in my projects), and I'll share some of my game design concept either before making a game or before storing it in a cardboard for the next decade, I need to keep some for later.

## What kind of games ?

I lake FPS (first person shooters like counter strike), RTS (real time strategy like starcraft), and MMOG (massively multiplayer online games), but in order to me to present you a mmo, you'll have to wait a little while.

I make games for me in the first place, this mean few things: **no boring stories,** it would be hard not to spoil myself the end if I'm the one writing it, **procedural generation** of levels (as far as I can go), so the game can surprise myself, and to enhance replayability, **multiplayer**, because there is nothing better than exploding peoples on your own game :), and extremely **difficult** and without tutorials, making hard game can be considered as a style, but with the few game design courses I got, I understand now the importance of a nice learning curve, preparing the player to handle the true levels, so this should be less true in my upcoming games.

Otherwise, I'm very interested in voxel terrain generation (like minecraft, to mention only one), I haven't yet got the time to implement it but as soon as I have a working engine, i'll use it in every games.

At last, I love play with physics(hook, jetpack, sliding, etc) and mobility in general(dash, short range teleportation, etc).
